    Running in the background
    A phone suspends any web page once you lock the screen or switch apps; no permission changes that. Keep awake (top bar) asks for permission to hold the screen on, and the desk trades for as long as it is open. For trading with your phone off, use the cloud autopilot that ships with this desk: the same engine runs on GitHub's servers every 15 minutes and places trades on your Deriv account, with stop and target enforced by Deriv itself. Any trade it opens appears here in Positions.
